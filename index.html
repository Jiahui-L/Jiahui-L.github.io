About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Internet of Things Thrust, Information Hub, at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Shijian Gao and co-advised by Prof. Yuxuan Liang. Before that, I obtained my bachelor’s degree from School of Information & Communication Engineering, at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in 2024.

Research Interests

My research interests focus on next-generation intelligent wireless networks design, including multi-dimensional radio field construction guided by real-world priors and physical-layer optimization enabled by ubiquitous multi-modal sensing. I am also exploring AI on RANs and embodied networks.
